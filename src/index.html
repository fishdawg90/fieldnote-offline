Phone test & troubleshooting
  1. Prepare while online. Keep this page open until the download completes.
  2. Run the sample test. It should transcribe “my fellow Americans”.
  3. In Chrome’s menu, choose Install app or Add to home screen.
  4. Turn on airplane mode. Close and reopen the app, then run the sample test again.
  5. Record 5–15 seconds in A4. Stop, wait for transcription, then export the files.

GPS can take longer without a network. Maps need internet. Keep the app open while recording or transcribing. Short clips work best; this proof of concept limits recordings to two minutes. Transcription accuracy and speed need checking on your phone.

Your field notebook

Media stays on this device. Back up regularly to keep a copy outside your browser.

GPS: original photo bytes and any existing EXIF are retained. Camera/browser settings may omit photo GPS; the app also saves a separate device location, time and accuracy. Export includes GPS JSON sidecars and a CSV index. JPEG photos without embedded GPS also get a separate geotagged copy where possible. Device positions are estimates, not survey coordinates.

Offline web app: prepare once over HTTPS, then use from your home screen without internet. Whisper Tiny English runs on this phone. Model files and runtime are cached; no API key is used. Browser or employer policies may still restrict microphone, GPS, installation or storage. Clearing this site’s data removes records and offline files. Export regularly.
